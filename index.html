Common questions

Choosing a hydroseeder in Melbourne.

The key decisions before you request a quote.

Which Vortec hydroseeder suits my application?

Start with the mulch type and application rate, then check tank capacity, carrier payload and the sites your crew will cover. The selection guide compares the VR and VX agitation systems.

Can I buy a hydroseeder for a Melbourne crew?

Yes. Vortec designs and manufactures hydroseeders in Australia. The range on this page includes compact VR units and the paddle-agitated VX2000, with skid and trailer options.

Which machine handles wood fibre mulch?

The VX-Series uses paddle agitation and handles wood fibre, paper and pourable mulches. The VR-Series uses jet agitation for paper and pourable mulches. Confirm your specified material with Vortec before selecting a machine.

Should I choose a skid or a trailer?

A skid unit mounts to a suitable carrier; a trailer gives you a dedicated towable configuration. Check vehicle capacity, access, transport requirements and how often the unit will move between sites.

What should I include in a quote enquiry?

Select a product or choose “Help me choose”, then share your business type, address and the material, application and carrier details in the message. .
