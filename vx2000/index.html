Overview

The VX2000 hydromulcher pairs aggressive, bidirectional paddle agitation with a high-flow gear pump. Its paddles break up and mix hydromulch bales, while the gear pump moves thick slurry through long hose runs.

Its poly tank, integrated bale tray and sloping floor help with loading and cleaning. Vortec lists large embankments, mine rehabilitation, roadside seeding and commercial landscaping among its applications.

VX2000 features

Made for thick
slurries and tough sites

The VX2000 combines paddle agitation, a gear pump, poly tank, bale tray and sloping floor.

01 / Agitation

Bidirectional paddles

High-speed mechanical agitation breaks up bales and keeps the entire load evenly mixed.

02 / Pump

High-flow gear pump

Vortec says its 2-inch gear pump can move thick hydromulch slurry through long hose runs.

03 / Tank

Corrosion-resistant poly

Vortec describes the poly tank as lightweight, durable and resistant to corrosion.

04 / Loading

Integrated bale tray

Space to prepare and load bales directly beside the mixing tank.

05 / Clean-out

Sloping tank floor

Vortec says the sloping floor allows slurry to drain from the tank.

06 / Operation

Left-hand operation

The VX2000 is operated from the left-hand side for roadside and traffic-area work.
